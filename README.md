# Awesome App Launch

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of launch boards, directories, checklists, tools and badges for launching an app.

This list covers places that take public app submissions, plus checklists, free tools and the badge mechanic many directories use. Every entry was checked in October 2026, but costs and link policies change, so confirm the details on each site before you submit. Additions are welcome through pull requests under the rules in the [Contributing](#contributing) section.

## Launch boards

- [AppLauncher](https://applauncher.dev/) - Curated app launch directory where a person reviews every submission against a published quality bar, with no account needed. Approved apps launch on a dated slot into a weekly feed and keep a permanent listing. Free submissions are reviewed and scheduled within 30 days, and the paid tiers Fast Track ($9) and Spotlight ($39) buy review within 24 hours and placement. Links start nofollow and turn dofollow through a verified badge on your site or an editor's pick.
- [BetaList](https://betalist.com) - Continuously published, editorially paced listings for pre-launch and recently launched startups. Paid entry only, with no free queue.
- [buildhop.io](https://buildhop.io/) - Startup distribution and launch platform with discovery, leaderboards and a tracker of other launch directories. Account needed and submissions are reviewed before going live; free tier, paid options. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [builtbyindies.com](https://builtbyindies.com/) - Weekly launch platform for indie hackers. Account needed, and free launches wait for the next open week unless you add their badge; free tier, paid options. Says free launches get a dofollow link only if they finish in the top three, while paid plans include dofollow links.
- [Dev Hunt](https://devhunt.org) - Launch platform for developer tools, suited to tools with a public repository. The free queue is long, and there is a paid option.
- [Fazier](https://fazier.com/) - Daily launch platform with paid self-scheduling. The free Basic tier has a review period and requires a Fazier badge on your homepage or footer; paid tiers also available. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [hunt0.com](https://hunt0.com/) - Community-voted weekly launches, with a list of other launch directories. Sign-in needed, and the free queue is long, while a badge launch skips it; free tier, paid options. Says dofollow goes to the weekly top 20, badge launches and premium launches.
- [ignlab.net](https://ignlab.net/) - Ignlab Launch, a launchpad for SaaS and indie tools. No account needed, every submission is reviewed by hand, and the homepage URL must be a bare domain or subdomain. Free. Links are nofollow.
- [indieneed.com](https://www.indieneed.com/) - Daily launch board for indie makers with upvotes and reviews. Free launches get human review, with limited free spots a day and up to two active products on the free plan; free tier, paid options. Says free listings get a nofollow link and dofollow comes with a paid upgrade.
- [launchboosts.com](https://launchboosts.com/) - AI tools directory with a weekly Launch Race. Listings publish instantly with no review queue. Free. Says listings start nofollow and the top three of the weekly or monthly vote, or an editor upgrade, get dofollow.
- [launchfree.io](https://launchfree.io/) - The Runway, a launch feed with a weekly newsletter. No account needed, a multi-step form with email, and human review in about a day. Free. Says approved listings get a permanent dofollow link.
- [LaunchingNext](https://www.launchingnext.com/) - Continuously published launch site with manual editorial review. Free with a queue that makers report as long, plus a paid option for consideration within one business day. Free listings are permanent and dofollow.
- [launchzone.co](https://launchzone.co/) - Weekly launch directory that also lists backlink tools and launch sites. Sign-in needed, and the free tier is reviewed in order; free tier, paid options. Dofollow on the free tier only while their badge stays on your site, and paid listings get dofollow without the badge.
- [MicroLaunch](https://microlaunch.net/) - Monthly launch cycle with community voting and feedback, plus Launch Anytime on paid tiers. Free launch, paid Launch Pro. Says Launch Pro and above get a lifetime dofollow link.
- [nextbigproduct.com](https://nextbigproduct.com/) - Launch platform with permanent product pages and scheduled launch dates, up to 10 launches a day. Sign-in needed, and AI drafts the listing; free tier, paid options.
- [openhunts.com](https://openhunts.com/) - Weekly launch board in the Product Hunt style. Account needed, and free launches wait in a long queue unless you add their badge; free tier, paid options. Says free launches get dofollow only if they finish in the top three, and paid launches get dofollow.
- [peerpush.com](https://peerpush.com/) - Product discovery and launch platform with upvotes and a directory comparison section. Account needed, the listing is drafted from your URL for you to review, and free listings queue behind paid ones; free tier, paid options. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/) - Daily launches starting at 12:01 AM Pacific, best for products with a real story and an audience you have already warmed up. Needs a personal account. Free to submit, with no paid queue.
- [productperch.com](https://productperch.com/) - Daily launch platform for software and digital products. Account needed, with 12 free slots a day and one free launch per domain every 30 days; free tier, paid options. Says links are dofollow when paid, open source, top three or badge verified, and nofollow otherwise.
- [rankinpublic.xyz](https://rankinpublic.xyz/) - Weekly niche tournaments decided by votes. Account needed, and free entries go through a vote-qualifying round; free tier, paid options. Dofollow for winners and badge-verified products.
- [sideprojects.net](https://sideprojects.net/) - Daily launchpad for side projects. Account needed, and free launches wait in a queue of usually a few weeks; free tier, paid options. Says the free launch includes a dofollow link.
- [spotstartups.com](https://spotstartups.com/) - Weekly launch board with batches ranked by votes. Account needed, with sign-in through Google or email. Free. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [TinyLaunch](https://www.tinylaunch.com/) - Fixed weekly launch periods with a public countdown. Standard Launch is free with several days on the homepage, plus a paid Premium Launch. Says free launches are nofollow unless they finish in the top three with the badge installed.
- [Uneed](https://www.uneed.best/) - Daily launches at midnight Pacific, with a few dozen products a day. The free queue assigns a date automatically and can run far out, while paid Fast-track and Skip the Line options let you pick a date. Says links are dofollow at 20 or more upvotes, nofollow at 10 to 19, and removed under 10.
- [worthtotry.com](https://worthtotry.com/) - AI tools directory with a weekly launch board and a page of other launch directories. Account by magic link, and every submission is reviewed with a readiness audit; free tier, paid options. Says the free launch includes a dofollow link with no badge required.

## Startup and side project directories

- [indietools.app](https://www.indietools.app/) - Directory of indie products and founders. Account needed, and free listings need the IndieTools badge verified on your site before manual review; free tier, paid options. Dofollow once their badge is on your site, and paid plans get a permanent dofollow link.
- [promoteproject.com](https://www.promoteproject.com/) - Startup directory with articles, jobs and a forum. Account needed, registered with email. Free. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [shareyourstartup.com](https://shareyourstartup.com/) - Startup list with upvotes and no signup. You submit a name, URL, one-line pitch and email, and the wait is shorter if you share the site or add their badge; free tier, paid options. Links are nofollow.
- [sideprojectors.com](https://www.sideprojectors.com/) - Marketplace to sell, buy or showcase side projects such as SaaS, websites, blogs, apps and domains. Account needed; free tier, paid options.
- [startup-list.org](https://startup-list.org/) - Permanent listings for startups, AI and SaaS products, plus a guide to startup directories. Account needed, with hand review in 12 to 48 hours, which their badge or a paid boost can skip; free tier, paid options. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [startupbuffer.com](https://startupbuffer.com/) - Startup directory organized by market and location, online since 2015. No account needed, and you submit a name, URL, business email, pitch, short description and screenshot for review; free tier, paid options. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.

## Software and SaaS directories

- [azn8.com](https://azn8.com/) - Directory of SaaS, startup and AI tools, including other directories. Free account by email verification or Google, with no badge, backlink or phone required, and submission also works by API or MCP. Free. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [citablehub.com](https://citablehub.com/) - AI-readable software directory that lists many kinds of projects. New listings show as pending review. Free. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [curateclick.com](https://curateclick.com/) - Hand-reviewed catalog of developer tools and products that also links out to other directories. The free tier has a wait, and paid tiers get instant approval; free tier, paid options. Dofollow once their badge is on your site, and paid tiers get dofollow.
- [everlist.dev](https://everlist.dev/) - SaaS discoverability platform that generates listing, alternatives and comparison pages. Account needed, live SaaS only, with an eligibility review before going live; free tier, paid options. Says listings carry a crawlable dofollow link.
- [findly.tools](https://findly.tools/) - Curated tools directory with a Places to submit section. Sign-in needed, and not every submission is accepted; free tier, paid options. Dofollow once their badge is on your site, as long as it stays there.
- [goodfirms.co](https://www.goodfirms.co/) - B2B reviews platform for software products and service agencies. Account needed, with multi-step research verification by their team and a low acceptance rate; free tier, paid options. Links are nofollow.
- [hicyou.com](https://hicyou.com/) - SaaS directory that is part of a network of navigation sites. Account needed through GitHub, Google or email, with limited free slots a day; free tier, paid options. Says badge verification unlocks extra slots and dofollow, and paid tiers get dofollow across the network.
- [indiehackerstacks.com](https://indiehackerstacks.com/) - Showcase of indie hacker products and their tech stacks. Account needed, with the add button behind login; free tier, paid options. Says the free tier includes a backlink.
- [makeitla.st](https://www.makeitla.st/) - Permanent software catalog that also lists launch sites and directories, and accepts agencies and studios. Account through Google or magic link, with an AI-drafted card and automatic review on save; free tier, paid options. Dofollow once their badge is on your site, or with payment.
- [neeed.directory](https://neeed.directory/) - Curated tool directory with its own Directories category. Sign-in needed, with an automatic check before going live; free tier, paid options. Dofollow once their badge is on your site, or with a paid permanent option.
- [postlaunchkit.com](https://postlaunchkit.com/) - Curated directory of free indie tools. No sign-in, one live indie product with a usable free option, and human review. Free. Says listings become followed once their badge or any link to the site is on your homepage.
- [publishyoursaas.com](https://publishyoursaas.com/) - Curated directory for indie and SaaS products, with a Directories category. Sign-in needed, and free review can take weeks or longer; free tier, paid options. Dofollow once their badge is on your site, and it is checked daily.
- [resource.fyi](https://resource.fyi/) - Curated resources and tools for developers and designers. Social sign-in through Google or GitHub, then submit from the header for team review. Free. Links are nofollow.
- [saashub.com](https://www.saashub.com/) - Large software alternatives directory that also keeps a list of launch directories. Account needed, for released English-language products on their own domain, with competitors listed and no agencies or waitlist pages; verifying the product raises priority in the free review queue. Free tier, paid options. Links are nofollow.
- [tolodora.com](https://tolodora.com/) - Launch and discovery platform for software and services. Listings go live on submit with no review queue; free tier, paid options. Says the free link is standard nofollow.
- [toolradar.com](https://toolradar.com/) - Database of B2B software pricing, reviews and alternatives. Account needed, B2B software only, with automated checks for factual copy, a working logo and site, and comparable pricing. Free. Links are nofollow.
- [worktoolmap.com](https://worktoolmap.com/) - Task-based directory of online tools. No account needed, just a name, website and email, with private editorial review and no guarantee of publication. Free. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.

## App directories

- [apprater.net](https://apprater.net/) - Human-reviewed archive of web, iOS, Android and desktop apps. No account needed, a homepage form with name, email and app URL, and review before it appears. Free. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [getprojectradar.com](https://getprojectradar.com/) - Curated index of independent projects and AI skills with editorial Project Notes. Needs a public page or repository, with editorial review and no guaranteed publication; payment never buys placement. Free. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [scoutforge.net](https://scoutforge.net/) - App discovery and review platform for web and mobile apps. Sign-in needed; free tier, paid options. Says a paid Featured upgrade adds a permanent dofollow link.

## AI tool directories

- [aicoming.net](https://aicoming.net/) - AI tools directory. Account needed, with submission through a login-gated dashboard; free tier, paid options. Says free listings are nofollow and a paid plan adds a permanent dofollow link.
- [aiindigo.com](https://aiindigo.com/) - AI tools directory that also covers MCP servers and agents. Web form with optional email, listed after review. Free.
- [aimarketly.com](https://www.aimarketly.com/) - Marketplace for AI tools, prompts, skills, MCP servers and agents. No account needed, email required, and human review within 48 hours. Free. Says approved listings get a permanent dofollow link.
- [dang.ai](https://dang.ai/) - Large AI tools directory. Email account needed, and the free tier requires a crawlable backlink to dang.ai and one category, while paid tiers skip the backlink; free tier, paid options. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [tulimoa.com](https://tulimoa.com/) - Hand-reviewed directory for AI and MCP software in English and German. Account through Google or email, with their badge on your site for the free listing and a listing within 7 days; free tier, paid options. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.

## Developer, MCP and agent directories

- [llmstxt.info](https://llmstxt.info/) - German directory of organizations' llms.txt files. Your site must publish an llms.txt, which their tool can generate, with editorial review in 48 hours, or 24 hours on the paid tier; free tier, paid options. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [mcppedia.com](https://mcppedia.com/) - Encyclopedia of MCP servers drawn from the official registry, GitHub, npm and PyPI. Account needed, submit from the dashboard with a repo, package or remote URL, then quality checks and moderator review. Free. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.
- [mcpservers.org](https://mcpservers.org/) - Awesome MCP Servers directory. Must be an MCP server with a repo, website or docs and a contact email, with no account shown; free tier, paid options. Says the dofollow link comes only with the paid Premium Submit.
- [nothumansearch.ai](https://nothumansearch.ai/) - Search index of agent-ready websites. You submit a site URL only, and the site is crawled and scored on agent-readiness signals such as llms.txt, OpenAPI, MCP and schema. Free. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.

## Website directories

- [develif.com](https://develif.com/) - General website directory across many categories. No account and no queue, just a URL, email, description and category, published instantly. Free. Says approved listings get a dofollow link.

## Badges

A launch badge is a small image a directory gives you to place on your own site, linking back to your listing. Several directories above use it as a reciprocal mechanic: the free listing's link turns dofollow only while their badge stays on your site. Here is an example of the pattern, using AppLauncher's Markdown badge snippet:

```markdown
[![YOUR APP NAME launched on applauncher.dev](https://applauncher.dev/badge/YOUR-SLUG.svg)](https://applauncher.dev/apps/YOUR-SLUG/)
```

## Contributing

Additions are welcome. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and follow the [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Each addition must meet these rules:

- The site takes public submissions from anyone.
- The site is live.
- The entry is one or two neutral sentences in the standard format.
- Entries stay alphabetical within their section.
- No prices for third-party sites.
- No affiliate or referral links.
- Pull requests that only promote the submitter's own site without meeting these rules are closed.
