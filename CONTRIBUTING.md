# Contributing

Additions, corrections and removals are welcome as pull requests. A maintainer checks every entry before it is merged.

## What qualifies

- The site takes public submissions from anyone. Invite-only sites and private lists do not qualify.
- The site is live and accepting submissions at the time you open the pull request.
- The site lists apps, software, websites, startups or developer tools, or it is a checklist, guide, tool or badge for launching one.
- The site is not a paid-only placement with no free tier, unless it is widely used.
- The entry is not a personal blog post or a single tweet.

## How to write an entry

- Write one line in this format:

  ```
  - [Name](https://homepage/) - One or two sentences.
  ```

- In the description, say what the site is and how submission works: whether you need an account, and whether listings are reviewed or go live instantly. Say whether the site is free, has a free tier or is paid only. Do not include a price.
- Describe the link policy only as the site itself states it.
- Place the entry in alphabetical order within its section.
- Link to the site's homepage. Do not use a referral or affiliate URL, and do not link to your own listing on the site.
- Add one entry per pull request, unless the entries are related.

## What gets closed

- Entries that only promote your own site and do not meet the rules above.
- Prices, domain ratings or other claims that cannot be checked on the site itself.
- Marketing language, such as "best," "leading" or "number one."
- Changes that reorder entries or edit other entries without a stated reason.

## Reporting a dead or changed site

Open an issue with the site's URL and a short note on what changed. If a site stops taking submissions, its entry is removed rather than marked.

## Updating your own entry

Open a pull request with the change you want to make. Include a link to the page on your site that shows the change, so a maintainer can confirm it.
